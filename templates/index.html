Best 6-Piece Combination

The optimizer evaluates thousands of possible gear combinations and selects the one with the highest total weighted score.

Pieces score is the sum of individual piece scores. Set bonuses are added when 2, 4, or 6 pieces from the same tier are included.

The score is weighted — buffs you ranked as high priority in the Mapping sheet contribute more to the score than lower-priority buffs.

Click any piece name to see its full stats. Click ⇄ Compare to compare this recommendation against your current gear.

Collective Stats

Raw % is the total percentage from all 6 pieces combined (including set bonuses) for that stat.

Wtd Pts is the weighted score contribution — Raw % multiplied by the priority weight you set in the Mapping sheet. This is what drives the ranking.

Stats are sorted: explicitly weighted items first (highest pts to lowest), then unweighted items shown as N/A.

Hover any stat row to see a tooltip showing which pieces contributed to it.

Green = buff  |  Red = debuff  |  Amber = flat value (not a percentage)
